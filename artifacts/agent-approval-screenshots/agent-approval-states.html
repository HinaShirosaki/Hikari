BLI Binding of a Biotinylated Peptide to YEATS2-His on Streptavidin Sensors

Purpose

Measure binding of a test peptide to purified YEATS2-His by biolayer interferometry using a biotinylated peptide immobilized on streptavidin biosensors and soluble YEATS2-His as the analyte.

Materials

  • N-terminally biotinylated test peptide with a short spacer
  • Purified YEATS2-His
  • Streptavidin BLI biosensors such as SA or SAX
  • Kinetics buffer: 20 mM HEPES pH 7.4, 150 mM NaCl, 0.01% Tween-20, 0.1% BSA
  • Buffer-matched blank and unloaded-sensor controls

Steps

  1. Hydrate the streptavidin biosensors in kinetics buffer for at least 10 minutes.
  2. Prepare a peptide loading solution and adjust to a 0.2–0.8 nm target response.
  3. Run a two-fold YEATS2-His dilution series from 0.156 to 10 µM with reference subtraction.
  4. Inspect raw traces and fit steady state first; use 1:1 kinetic fitting only for clean, ordered curves.
