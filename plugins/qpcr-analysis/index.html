ΔCt = mean target Ct − mean reference Ct. ΔΔCt = sample ΔCt − mean calibrator ΔCt. Fold change = 2−ΔΔCt. This model assumes approximately equal target and reference efficiencies near 100%.

Fit mean Ct against log₁₀ starting quantity, weighting each quantity equally. Efficiency = (10−1/slope − 1) × 100%.
